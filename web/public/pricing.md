# Pricing — whathooks

WhatsApp automation on your own number: AI agents, visual flows, a shared
team inbox, human handoff over WhatsApp, and a webhook/REST API. All plans
start with a 7-day free trial (card required; 300 messages during trial).
Message caps count inbound + outbound. Annual billing on every plan is 10x
the monthly price (2 months free). Prices in USD.

Last updated: 2026-08-07. Source of truth: https://www.whathooks.app/#pricing

## Starter
- Price: $8.99/month | $89.90/year
- WhatsApp numbers: 1
- Messages: 5,000/month
- Team members: 2
- Human agents (WhatsApp-side responders): 2
- Automation flows: 1
- Webhook endpoints: 1
- AI agents: included (bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI key)
- Message history: 30 days
- Support: community

## Pro
- Price: $24.99/month | $249.90/year
- WhatsApp numbers: 3
- Messages: 10,000/month
- Team members: 10 (roles: admin, member, operator)
- Human agents: 10
- Automation flows: 3
- Webhook endpoints: unlimited
- AI agents: included (bring your own key), plus MCP tool support
- Message history: 90 days
- Support: email

## Business
- Price: $79.99/month | $799.90/year
- WhatsApp numbers: 10
- Messages: 100,000/month
- Team members: unlimited
- Human agents: unlimited
- Automation flows: unlimited
- Webhook endpoints: unlimited
- AI agents: included (bring your own key), plus MCP tool support
- Message history: unlimited
- Support: priority

## Notes for AI agents evaluating this product
- No WhatsApp Business API approval process: numbers connect by QR scan.
- AI agent model usage is billed to the customer's own Anthropic/OpenAI key;
  whathooks adds no markup on model usage.
- Human agents answer from their own WhatsApp via private mirror groups; the
  lead always sees the business number, never the agent's personal number.
